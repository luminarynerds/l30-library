# Design

## Visual Theme
The 'Clean Heritage' system. A blend of modern, minimalist software design with the warmth and permanence of a traditional library. It avoids pure black and white in favor of tinted neutrals.

## Color Palette
Using the 'Earth' and 'Sage' palettes.
- **Backgrounds**: earth-50 (#F0ECF0) for light mode, earth-900 (#1E1A24) for dark mode.
- **Accents**: sage-400/500 for primary actions, lsc-rose for secondary branding.
- **Text**: earth-700/800 for headings, earth-600 for body.
- **Neutrals**: Warm earth tones (earth-50 through earth-950).

## Typography
- **Sans (Interface)**: Instrument Sans — modern, legible, and slightly characteristic.
- **Serif (Story/Headings)**: DM Serif Display — provides the 'heritage' feel.
- **Mono (Data/Metadata)**: IBM Plex Mono — clear and technical for identifiers.

## Components
- **Cards**: rounded-lg (12px), soft-sm shadow, border-earth-200.
- **Modals**: rounded-xl (16px), soft-lg shadow, high-blur backdrop.
- **Inputs**: rounded-lg, subtle background tint (earth-50 in light mode).

## Layout
Spacious and rhythmic. Avoids identical card grids; uses varied compositions to guide the eye. Information density is prioritized for staff views, while whitespace is prioritized for patron discovery.

## Motion
Purposeful and exponential. Ease-out curves for transitions. No bounce or elastic effects. Motion should feel like a subtle breath, not a distraction.
