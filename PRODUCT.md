# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Patrons of all ages (from children to seniors) and library staff (librarians, directors, and administrators). Patrons use the system for discovery and account management, while staff use it for high-velocity circulation and complex administration.

## Product Purpose
L30 is an open-source Integrated Library System (ILS) designed to replace fragmented legacy library software with a unified, modern, and accessible digital hub. It serves as the operating system for a physical and digital library community.

## Brand Personality
Comfortable, Reliable, Quiet, and Background. It should feel like a trusted, familiar tool that is always working correctly without demanding constant attention.

## Anti-references
Cluttered 2000s catalog interfaces, overly aggressive 'SaaS-cream' startup aesthetics, and cold, impersonal government portals.

## Design Principles
1. **Accessibility First**: Deep neurodivergent and physical accessibility is the core identity, not an after-thought. 
2. **Invisible Utility**: The interface should feel like 'wearing a glove'—a natural, ergonomic extension of the user's intent that 'hums in the background.'
3. **Sensory Comfort**: Utilizing the warm 'Earth' palette and purposeful, exponential motion to create a calm, reliable sensory environment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as a baseline, with specific focus on neuro-inclusive features like quiet modes, focus modes, and high-contrast/dyslexia-friendly overlays.
